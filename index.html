01 / About

Who am I?

I'm a Virtual Assistant with a Computer Engineering background, and I use that technical side to make admin work run smoother. I look for ways to make tasks more efficient and avoid unnecessary repetition.

From March 2024 to February 2025, I supported a Canadian law firm remotely: managing client files in Clio, drafting documents from templates, and researching legislation. For that research, I built my own tracker to match each claim to its source, so nothing got lost between drafts. More recently, I've helped with legal admin work by setting up a monthly reconciliation tracker and cleaning up a broken file-sync system. I renamed instead of deleting, because the people who know the files best should decide what goes.

My rule is simple: if I'll do something more than ten times, I build a template for it. I set the standard before I start, automate what's repetitive, and keep the judgment calls manual. My goal is to use my technical experience to help teams in different fields get more done with less effort.
