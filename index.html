01 / About

Who am I?

I am a Computer Engineer from the Philippines with a background in networking and systems administration. Across the work I build and study, I look for ways to make tasks more efficient and avoid unnecessary repetition. For my thesis, I integrated Ghidra and GDB so reverse engineering work did not require manually switching between them.

I bring that same instinct to client work. After spending the past year as a remote Virtual Assistant for a Canadian law firm, I am continuing to grow through technical support, administration, and workflow-focused work. My goal is to use my technical experience to help teams in different fields get more done with less effort.
