Who am I?

I am a Computer Engineer from the Philippines, with a background in Networking & Systems Administration. There's a pattern across most of what I build and study: finding ways to do things once instead of repeatedly — my thesis, for example, synced Ghidra and GDB so reverse engineering work didn't require manually switching back and forth between them. I bring that same instinct to client work: I spent the past year as a remote Virtual Assistant for a Canadian law firm, and I'm now expanding into technical IT support, aiming to help the people I work with get more done with less effort. I continue growing through hands-on projects like this portfolio.
